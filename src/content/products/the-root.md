Look at an old tree in a storm. The branches thrash. Leaves tear away by the hundreds. It looks like the whole thing is about to come apart. And after the storm, it's still standing.

Not because the branches were strong. Because of something you couldn't see at all.

This book is about that something. You don't have to build it or earn it. You only have to learn the way back, again and again, in the moments you lose it.
