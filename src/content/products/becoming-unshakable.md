A short, practical and quietly radical book on the oldest idea in Taoism: strength that does not push.

It is written for people who never read the Tao Te Ching. Each part takes one old principle (yielding, water, softness, emptiness, stillness, doing less, knowing when to step back) and shows how it changes an ordinary day.
