You've tried to be strong by getting harder. More control. More effort. Thicker walls. And still, one message, one argument, one bad morning can knock you over.

Two and a half thousand years ago Laozi saw why. The stiff tree is broken by the storm. The grass bends, and is still standing in the morning.

This book is built on that one image. You become unshakable when you stop fighting what you don't need to fight. It shows you how, in ten minutes a day, with the honesty to notice when you are fighting.
