# Silent Power | Tao Te Ching

> Silent Power is an independent project by Yod Mem that makes the Tao Te Ching accessible without making it shallow: short illustrated books, free guides, music and videos in plain language. Books are sold and delivered as PDFs through Gumroad. It is not related to audio or computer-hardware brands with a similar name.

The books themselves are written in English. The Tao Te Ching chapter pages are available in English and Hebrew. The site also has translated pages (descriptions only) in Spanish, French, Italian, Hindi and Filipino. Hebrew has its own Hebrew-language books.

## Books
- [The Taoist Secret to Becoming Unshakable](https://silentpower.me/shop/becoming-unshakable/): 84-page illustrated book, 16 chapters in 7 parts, $4. Method: See it. Name it. Let it be.
- [The Taoist Secret of the Root](https://silentpower.me/shop/the-root/): 80-page book with nine guided MP3 audios, $4.
- [The Way of the Tao: Stop Pushing So Hard](https://silentpower.me/shop/stop-pushing/): free 21-page guide, about a 10-minute read.
- [The Way of the Tao: When Your Mind Won't Slow Down](https://silentpower.me/shop/mind-wont-slow-down/): free guide plus a 5-minute guided audio.
- [The Sound of the Tao](https://silentpower.me/shop/sound-of-the-tao/): music album, 12 tracks, about 56 minutes, $9.
